============================================================
BLACK HOLE VISUALIZATION (blackhole-demo.html)
============================================================

Pure WebGL fragment shader:
- Event Horizon (Schwarzschild radius Rs)
- Ray-marching with 3D camera rotation matrices
- Accretion disk with 5-layer FBM noise (filamentary texture)
- Kepler rotation (ω ∝ r^(-1.5))
- Gravitational lensing:
  - Photon ring (ultra-thin, ultra-bright)
  - Einstein rings (2 layers)
  - Top and bottom arcs (far-side disk bent around BH)
- Color temperature gradient (white-hot → gold → amber → dark red)
- Doppler shift (approaching side brighter/bluer)
- Planet with rim lighting (scale reference)
- Two-layer starfield
- Mouse orbit interaction
